# 10. Government Project Proposal and Technical Quotation

**Supplier:** ApexiveAI (proposed)  
**Product:** Police Citizen Connect  
**Document status:** Preliminary ROM (rough-order-of-magnitude), not a tender
submission, contract, legal opinion or government endorsement  
**Currency:** USD; no MMK exchange rate assumed  
**Valid for:** Planning only; re-estimate after discovery and procurement
requirements are approved

## 10.1 Executive proposal

Propose a phased, privacy-led public-service platform: responsive citizen
information and request experiences, a scoped staff service-operations
portal, public content and directory management, and audited identity,
notification, document and reporting foundations.

The first release is a limited pilot for explicitly selected services and
offices. National rollout and high-risk police-internal modules are not
included automatically. Proceed only with a named government sponsor,
validated legal authority, independent DPIA/HRIA/security review, service
staffing, operating procedures, procurement approval and user testing.

## 10.2 Delivery scope

### Proposed pilot

- Discovery, service mapping, data inventory and architecture validation.
- OIDC/MFA, RBAC/scope, office/service directory and audit foundation.
- Citizen bilingual responsive portal and mobile app shell.
- Public information and verified contacts.
- Non-emergency request intake, acknowledgement, scoped staff queue,
  citizen-visible status and notification adapters.
- Limited appointment flow if pilot service owner is ready.
- Admin service/content configuration, aggregate metrics, privacy requests.
- CI/CD, monitoring, backups, restore drill, security test and training.

### Separately gated / future

Personnel/HR, formal police case management, evidence or digital forensics,
traffic citations, cybercrime investigations, court integrations, police
clearance issuance, emergency dispatch, identity/payment integrations,
procurement/finance, fleet/assets and any AI assistance. Each requires a
separate authorization, impact assessment, design and estimate.

### Explicitly excluded

Mass surveillance, biometric/facial recognition, background location,
citizen/suspect risk scoring, political or peaceful-assembly monitoring,
automated legal/enforcement decisions, unrestricted cross-database searches,
unverified emergency-response claims, and operations without a responsible
agency owner.

## 10.5 Commercial assumptions and payment

- Contract in USD or mutually agreed currency; exchange rate and tax treatment
  set at contract date.
- Work begins only after signed statement of work, procurement approval,
  initial governance gate and agreed payment schedule.
- Suggested milestone billing: 10% mobilization; 15% discovery acceptance;
  20% foundation; 20% citizen pilot; 20% staff workflow; 10% security/UAT;
  5% handover. Final schedule to follow tender terms.
- Change requests require impact statement, updated estimate and written
  approval before work.
- Source code, intellectual-property ownership, data-controller duties,
  hosting, support, warranty and liability must be specified in contract.
- No real data is used for development/UAT; synthetic fixtures only.

## 10.6 Deliverables and handover

- Approved SRS, architecture, ERD/data dictionary, OpenAPI, role matrix and
  UX specifications.
- Source, migrations, infrastructure-as-code, CI/CD definitions, SBOM and
  deployment/runbook documentation.
- Test evidence, security findings/remediation, restore drill and training.
- Admin guide, citizen help content and approved bilingual notices.
- Data retention/deletion runbook and independent oversight handover.

## 10.7 Governance and procurement conditions precedent

Prior to award or collection:

1. Verify supplier eligibility, procurement path, beneficial ownership and
   conflict-of-interest requirements through formal procurement.
2. Obtain written authorization from the responsible service authority.
3. Appoint data controller, service owners, privacy lead, security lead and
   independent oversight.
4. Complete local legal review of current primary sources; do not rely on
   summaries in the supplied brief.
5. Approve DPIA, HRIA, emergency/fallback plan, retention and correction
   rights.
6. Agree data residency, hosting ownership, subcontractors and support access.
7. Confirm citizen notification, complaints, accessibility and alternative
   channels are staffed.

## 10.8 Success measures

Establish pilot baselines first, then agree targets for successful request
submission, acknowledgement time, service completion time, directory
freshness, accessibility, citizen satisfaction, system availability, audit
coverage and recovery. Do not use arrest, detention, accusation, complaint
volume or individual citizen risk as staff performance targets.

## 10.9 Approval

This ROM is for planning and discussion only. It becomes a quotation only
after an approved discovery phase, procurement review, site/data-residency
decisions, validated quantities, final scope, service-level agreement and
written authorization. No government relationship or approval is implied.
