## Status and use

These are planning documents based on the supplied product brief. They are
not evidence of a government contract, agency endorsement, legal authority,
approved operational procedure, or deployed public service. The linked
product page is a static concept preview. Do not enter real citizen,
personnel, complaint, case, evidence, identity, or emergency data into it.

Before any pilot or production procurement, the responsible authorities and
independent reviewers must validate scope, legal basis, service ownership,
data protection, human-rights impacts, security controls, retention periods,
hosting jurisdiction, accessibility, language, and emergency contact details.
All Myanmar legal references in the supplied brief require verification by
qualified local counsel against current primary sources.

## Non-negotiable exclusions

The proposed product is for public information and accountable service
administration. It must not be used to build or enable:

- Mass surveillance, political-opinion or peaceful-assembly monitoring.
- Unrestricted personal-record searches or background location tracking.
- Mass facial recognition, biometric identification, or secret profiling.
- Automated citizen/suspect risk scoring or arrest, detention, or guilt
  recommendations.
- Automated legal outcomes, case closure, eligibility denial, or disciplinary
  decisions without a responsible human decision-maker.
- Public access to police-internal records, evidence, witness data, or another
  person's submissions.

Any future feature that could materially affect safety, liberty, privacy, or
access to a public service requires a new legal, security, and human-rights
review and explicit written approval before design or implementation.

## Planning assumptions

- The first release is a limited, opt-in pilot; it is not a nationwide
  deployment.
- Public information and service-directory entries are reviewed and approved
  by their designated owners before publication.
- Emergency contact information is informational unless an agency-approved,
  monitored integration is separately commissioned and tested.
- Citizen-facing and police-administration data stores are separated by API,
  authorization boundary, and purpose; public services do not query internal
  police databases directly.
- The indicative quotation is a ROM estimate in USD, not a binding tender
  offer. It excludes taxes, third-party fees, devices, and ongoing hosting.
